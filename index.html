这和算命有什么不同？

算命给你一个不可改变的结论，心镜给你一个可以行动的位置。它不预测事件与日期，只说「象如此，心如何」，并把决定权还给你。

会说我「命不好」吗？

不会。禁用凶、灾、破财、小人、刑冲克害这类词。星图里没有「坏配置」，只有你还没认领的部分。

不能。这是心性观照，不是医疗、心理治疗或诊断。有持续痛苦请找专业人士。

因为讲完星象容易飘。颜色让你今天就有事可做：穿什么、桌面上放什么、灯光换成什么。一次只给一个动作。

非托管：脚本在你本机运行，出生信息不写日志、不外传。

FAQ

How is this different from fortune-telling?

Fortune-telling hands you an unchangeable verdict. XinJing hands you a position you can act from. It predicts no events or dates; it says 'the image is thus — what does your heart say', and returns the decision to you.

Do I need my birth time?

For the full structure (ascendant, moon, houses, transits) yes, plus place. A date alone still works, but moon and ascendant will be approximate, and the page says so.

Will it tell me my fate is bad?

No. Words like disaster, ruin, loss, enemies, or malefic aspects are forbidden. There is no bad placement in a chart — only parts you haven't claimed yet.

Can it treat illness?

No. This is reflection, not medicine, therapy, or diagnosis. For lasting distress, see a professional.

Why colours?

Because astrology floats. Colour gives you something to do today: what to wear, what to put on your desk, how to change the light. One action at a time.

Is my data safe?

Non-custodial: the script runs on your own machine, birth data is never logged or sent.
